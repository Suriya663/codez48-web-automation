Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

At NS Handlooms, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data you share with us. This policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you visit our website or use our platform for premium handloom sarees.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, and delivery address when you place an order. We also collect device information and location data (with your permission) to improve our delivery services.

3. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and deliver your saree orders efficiently.
  • To provide live tracking updates via SMS and within the app.
  • To improve our website layout and product recommendations for premium handloom sarees.
  • To communicate with you regarding support requests or promotional offers.

4. Data Security

We implement a variety of security measures to maintain the safety of your personal information. Your payments are processed through secure, industry-standard gateways like Razorpay. We do not store your credit card or bank details on our servers.

5. Mobile Application

Our official Android application requires certain permissions (such as storage and location) to function correctly. These permissions are used solely for the purpose of order processing, file downloads (for digital products), and delivery tracking.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal details at any time through the "My Profile" section in Settings. If you wish to close your account, please contact our support team.

Return & Refund Policy

We want you to be completely satisfied with your purchase. If you encounter any issues, please review our return policy below:

  • 7-Day Return Window: You can request a return within 7 days of delivery for physical products.
  • Eligible Reasons: Returns are accepted for damaged products, wrong items received, or quality issues.
  • Process: Go to the "Track Order" section, find your order, and click "Return Product" to submit your request with a reason.
  • Refunds: Once your return is approved, we will process your refund to your original payment method or a provided UPI ID.
  • Digital Products: Courses and digital files are generally non-refundable once accessed, but please contact support for specific issues.
