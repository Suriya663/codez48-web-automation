Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about the Codez48 Pilot Android app.

Why does Android show "Install unknown apps"?
Because this APK is downloaded directly from the official Codez48 website rather than Google Play Store. This allows instant updates and raw Win32 input driver access. The APK is cryptographically signed and 100% secure.
How does the 6-Digit Email OTP work?
When you launch the app, type your email address and tap "Request OTP". A secure 6-digit verification code will be sent to your inbox. Enter the code to sign in instantly.
How do I connect my phone to my laptop cursor?
Run codez48 pilot on your laptop. On your mobile app, tap the Remote Mouse chip. Type your laptop's local IP or your Pilot Node ID and tap Connect. The touchpad surface is instantly active.
Does Gaming Gyro move the mouse cursor?
No. The mouse cursor is strictly controlled by your finger touching and sliding on the touchpad. The "Gaming Gyro" toggle enables dynamic Screen Auto-Rotation for wide landscape gaming controllers.
